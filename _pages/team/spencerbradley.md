Spencer Bradley is an undergraduate Aerospace Engineering student at the University of Maryland minoring in mathematics. He joined SPARC Lab in 2026 as a member of the University of Maryland Aerospace Honors Program with research interests including spacecraft state estimation and attitude control. His research focuses on attitude control of spacecraft using truss robots. Spencer has previously interned at AeroVironment, and will be interning next summer at Northrop Grumman Space Systems. Outside of the classroom, Spencer enjoys hiking, traveling with friends, cooking, watching baseball, and serves as Vice President of Theta Chi Fraternity at the University of Maryland.
