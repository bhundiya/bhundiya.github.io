T’ana Joseph is a Susan M. Clonan Fellow and an Aerospace Engineering Master’s student in SPARC Lab. Her current research focuses on structural dynamics and simulation development for AI-informed jitter prediction on mobile platforms. Her broader research interests include space systems engineering, spacecraft mission design, and planetary defense. She currently serves as the Deputy Principal Investigator for TERP RAPTOR, UMD’s deep-space CubeSat mission to asteroid Apophis, where she develops and refines mission architecture. Previously, T’ana optimized small-satellite constellations at JHU APL, prototyped Mars 2020 flight hardware at NASA JPL, studied smart polymers at the University of Freiburg, Germany, and modeled lunar impact trajectories at the University of Central Florida. She holds a B.S. in Mechanical Engineering from the University of Maryland, Baltimore County (UMBC). Outside of the lab, she enjoys writing short stories, producing music on her laptop, and discovering new cafes.
