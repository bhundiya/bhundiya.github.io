José Morel is a Clark Doctoral Fellow and an Aerospace Engineering PhD student in the SPARC Lab. His research interests include in-space servicing, assembly, and manufacturing (ISAM) technology concept development, as well as planetary surface robotics and structures. Prior to joining the SPARC Lab in 2026, José was an Aerospace Engineer within the Space Mission Analysis Branch at NASA Langley Research Center, where he worked on human spaceflight architectures and ISAM capability modeling. He also contributed to the development and testing of large-scale composite boom systems for solar sails and lunar infrastructure. José has completed internships at companies such as Redwire Space and Virgin Galactic, and was named a Matthew Isakowitz Fellow in 2023. He earned a Bachelor of Science in Mechanical Engineering from Binghamton University, as well as a Master of Science in Aerospace Engineering and a Graduate Certificate in Astrobiology, both from Georgia Tech. His work at Georgia Tech focused on aircraft/spacecraft concept design with the Aerospace Systems Design Lab. Outside of research, he enjoys hiking, climbing, and running triathlons.
