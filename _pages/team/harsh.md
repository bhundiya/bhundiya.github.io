Harsh is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Aerospace Engineering at the University of Maryland. He received a B.S. in Mechanical Engineering from the California Institute of Technology in 2020, and a Ph.D. in Aeronautics and Astronautics from the Massachusetts Institute of Technology in 2025. At MIT, Harsh worked in Prof. Zachary Cordero’s [Aerospace Materials and Structures Laboratory](http://cordero.mit.edu/), where his research focused on the rapid in-space assembly and manufacturing of large truss structures. He was a NASA Space Technology Graduate Research Fellow and a MIT School of Engineering Communication Fellow. After MIT, he was a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at the University of Southern California. He is a member of the AIAA Spacecraft Structures Technical Committee.
