Harsh Bhundiya is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Aerospace Engineering at the University of Maryland. He holds a Ph.D. in Aeronautics and Astronautics from the Massachusetts Institute of Technology in 2025 and a B.S. in Mechanical Engineering from the California Institute of Technology in 2020. At MIT, Harsh was a NASA Space Technology Graduate Research Fellow and worked in Prof. Zachary Cordero’s [Aerospace Materials and Structures Laboratory](http://cordero.mit.edu/), where his research focused on the rapid in-space assembly and manufacturing of large truss structures. After MIT, he was a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at the University of Southern California. He is a member of the AIAA Spacecraft Structures Technical Committee. Outside of research, he enjoys playing racket sports, hiking, and cooking.
