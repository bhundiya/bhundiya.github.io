Dr. Bhundiya gave an invited talk at the [Roadmap for In-Space Manufacturing of Resilient Structures](https://spacemanufacturing.illinois.edu/) workshop hosted by the University of Illinois Urbana-Champagin (UIUC) from May 19-20, 2026. The workshop focused on identifying and ranking challenges and opportunities for 1) in-space manufacturing and robotic assembly, 2) resilient space structures in low Earth orbit, 3) resilient space structures for lunar habitats, and 4) simulation and testing of the space environment.
